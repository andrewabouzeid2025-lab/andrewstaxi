The Bekaa is the heart of Lebanese wine. Book a **taxi to [Zahle](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator)** for a day of tastings and lunch by the river. Add **[Baalbek](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator)** and you can see the Roman temples and visit a winery on the same trip.

Batroun has wineries, seafood and a lively old town. Take a **taxi to [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator)**, then stop at **[Byblos (Jbeil)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator)** for the old souk and the harbour on the way back. This is an easy coastal day from Beirut.

### Mount Lebanon
Autumn is a good time for the mountains. Head to **[Faraya / Kfardebian](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator)** for fresh air and a long lunch, with a driver who knows the roads.
