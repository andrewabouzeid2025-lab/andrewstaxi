# Autumn Day Trip from Beirut: Jeita Grotto, Harissa and Byblos with a Private Driver

October brings milder weather to the Lebanese coast. The summer crowds have thinned and the light is softer. It is a good time for a day trip north of Beirut. The classic route links three well-known stops: **Jeita Grotto, Harissa and Byblos**. A Lebanon private driver is the easiest way to see all three in one day.

## Why do this trip by private driver?

The three stops are close to each other on the map. But public transport between them is limited, and the stops are not on a single line. Here is what a private driver gives you:

- **No waiting.** Your car goes where you go, on your schedule.
- **Fixed pricing.** You know the fare before you travel. There is no meter and no negotiating at the end.
- **Flexibility.** Stay longer in the Byblos souk or skip a stop if you are tired.
- **24/7 availability.** You can start early, or come back late after dinner by the sea.

## A suggested route

### 1. Start in Beirut or at the airport

Pick up from your hotel in [Beirut – Downtown / Achrafieh](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) or [Beirut – Hamra / Verdun / Ras Beirut](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). If you have just landed, we can collect you directly at [Beirut Airport (BEY)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). Your driver will wait for you, and you can begin the trip after you check in and freshen up.

### 2. Jeita Grotto

Jeita is a system of limestone caves with an underground river. It is one of the most visited natural sites in the country. Opening hours and access can change, especially in bad weather. Check the current schedule before you go, and ask your driver to confirm the timing.

### 3. Harissa, in the Jounieh area

From the caves, continue toward [Jounieh / Kaslik / Zouk](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). Harissa sits above the bay and is known for its panoramic views and the Our Lady of Lebanon statue. Go in the late morning or the afternoon for the clearest views over the coast. Jounieh and Kaslik also have many places for lunch or coffee.

### 4. Byblos (Jbeil)

End the day at [Byblos (Jbeil)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator), one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. Walk through the archaeological site and the old souk, then sit by the harbor. If you want to make the trip longer, you can continue to [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) for its old town, seafront and evening dining.

Asking for a **taxi to Byblos** is simple. Choose your pickup zone, check the fare in the fare estimator, and book.

## Evening plans: dinner and nightlife

Byblos and Batroun both have good seafood restaurants, pubs and late-night spots. You don't need to worry about driving or parking. Order your Andrews Taxi when you are ready to leave, and we will take you back to Beirut or to your hotel at any hour.

## Tips for a smooth day

1. **Book ahead.** Tell us your pickup zone, group size and how many stops you want.
2. **Start early.** You will have more daylight and calmer roads.
3. **Dress in layers.** The caves are cool and the coast can get breezy in the evening.
4. **Check official guidance.** Conditions in Lebanon can change. Before you travel, check your government's travel advice and local updates. Your driver can also adjust the route if needed.
5. **Keep your flight in mind.** If you leave the same day, tell us your departure time. We can plan the return to the airport with extra margin.
