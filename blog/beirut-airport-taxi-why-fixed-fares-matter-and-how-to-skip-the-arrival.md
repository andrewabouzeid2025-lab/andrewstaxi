# Beirut Airport Taxi: Why Fixed Fares Matter and How to Skip the Arrivals Hall Haggling

You land at Rafic Hariri International Airport after a long flight. Your bags are on the trolley, your phone is at 12%, and the next thing you face is a negotiation over the fare. For years this was the standard welcome for many visitors and returning expatriates.

The topic is now back in the news. Lebanon's Public Works Ministry launched a campaign to regulate public taxi fares from the airport to all regions of the country. Travelers and residents had gone about 11 years without unified pricing. Clear, published fares are good for everyone. They are also what we at Andrews Taxi have believed in since day one.
