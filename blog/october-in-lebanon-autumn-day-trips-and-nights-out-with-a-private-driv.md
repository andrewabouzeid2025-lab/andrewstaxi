# October in Lebanon: Autumn Day Trips and Nights Out With a Private Driver

## Day Trip Ideas for October

### 1. Byblos (Jbeil): Ancient Stones by the Sea
Byblos is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. In autumn you can walk the old souk, the castle and the harbour without the summer crowds. A **taxi to Byblos** from Beirut is an easy half-day or full-day trip. Your driver can wait while you explore, then take you to lunch by the port. Check the fare to [Byblos (Jbeil)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator).

### 2. Batroun: Seafood, Sunsets and Old Souks
Batroun is a little further up the coast. It has a charming old town, lemonade stands and seafood restaurants. Combine it with Byblos for a full coastal day, and you can head home after sunset without worrying about the drive. See the fare to [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator).

### 3. Faraya and Kfardebian: Mountain Air
Autumn colours and cooler air make the mountains pleasant before the ski season. Mountain roads are easier with a local driver who knows them well. Plan your ride to [Faraya / Kfardebian](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator).

### 4. Baalbek and Zahle: Roman Temples and Wine Country
Baalbek's temples are among the most impressive Roman ruins anywhere. Pair them with Zahle, known for its riverside restaurants and its wine culture. This is a longer day, so a comfortable private car is the best way to do it. Plan your trip to [Baalbek](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) and [Zahle](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator).

### 5. The South: Saida and Tyre
Saida (Sidon) has its sea castle and old souks. Tyre (Sour) has its Roman ruins and beaches. Both work well as a full day out from Beirut. Check fares to [Saida (Sidon)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) and [Tyre (Sour)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator).
