# Autumn Day Trip from Beirut: Byblos, Batroun and the Coast with a Private Driver

Autumn is one of the best times to see Lebanon's northern coast. September to November is mild and the crowds are smaller than in peak summer. October is a good month for walking old stone streets, eating by the sea and taking your time.

This is a simple one-day plan from Beirut to Byblos and Batroun. It works for visitors landing at the airport and for residents who want a day out.

## Why a Private Driver Works Best for This Route

The coastal highway north of Beirut is easy to drive, but a day trip has many stops: parking in old town centres, a long lunch, a sunset walk. With a **Lebanon private driver** you don't need to:

- Search for parking in busy souks.

- Negotiate prices at each stop.

## Suggested Itinerary

### 1. Morning: Pick-up in Beirut
Start from your hotel or home in [Beirut – Downtown / Achrafieh](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) or [Beirut – Hamra / Verdun / Ras Beirut](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). If you have just landed, we can meet you at [Beirut Airport (BEY)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) and take you straight on your way. A **Beirut airport taxi** booked in advance means no queue and no waiting.

### 2. Stop One: Jounieh and Harissa
Many visitors stop first in the [Jounieh / Kaslik / Zouk](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) area. From there you can visit Our Lady of Lebanon in Harissa, which has wide views over the bay. Your driver waits while you explore.

### 3. Midday: Byblos (Jbeil)
Ask for a **taxi to Byblos** and spend a few hours in [Byblos (Jbeil)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). Byblos is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. You can:

- Walk through the ancient archaeological site and the Crusader castle.
- Wander the old souk.
- Have lunch or fresh lemonade at the old port.

### 4. Afternoon: Batroun
Continue to [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) for the old Phoenician sea wall, the small souks and St. Stephen's Cathedral. Batroun is also known for its seaside cafés and its evening food and drink scene.

### 5. Evening: Dinner, Then Home
Stay for sunset and dinner. When you are ready, your driver takes you back to Beirut or to [Hazmieh / Baabda / Dekwaneh / Jdeideh](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator), with no need to drive after a long day or a drink.
