# Autumn in Lebanon: A Private Driver Day Trip from Beirut Airport to Byblos and Batroun

October is one of the best months to see Lebanon's coast. The summer crowds are gone and the heat has eased. The sea is still warm enough to enjoy from a terrace. Old stone streets, harbors and seaside restaurants are far more pleasant at this time of year.

Here is how to plan a simple, stress-free coastal day trip with a private driver.

## Stop One: Byblos (Jbeil)

Byblos is among the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. A **taxi to Byblos** from Beirut or the airport takes you up the coast in under an hour in normal traffic.

Plan to see:

- **The Crusader castle and archaeological site.** Phoenician, Roman and medieval layers sit side by side.
- **The old souk.** Handmade crafts, souvenirs and small cafés.
- **The old harbor.** Fresh seafood is served with a view of the fishing boats.

A private driver means you can park your luggage in the car, walk freely and leave when you are ready. You don't need to watch the clock for a bus.

## Stop Two: Batroun

Another short drive north is Batroun, a Phoenician coastal town with a lively old center. Autumn is a calm time to visit, and the town is easier to explore with fewer visitors.

Highlights include:

- **The old souks and the Phoenician sea wall.**
- **Local lemonade and seafood** in the small restaurants by the water.
- **The Nabu Museum** on the coast.
- **Wineries and craft beer.** The Batroun area is known for both. Because you have a driver, everyone in your group can enjoy a tasting.

In the evening Batroun has a busy pub and nightlife scene. You can order a taxi back to Beirut, Jounieh or your hotel when the night is over. Nobody has to drive, and you don't have to look for a ride at midnight.
