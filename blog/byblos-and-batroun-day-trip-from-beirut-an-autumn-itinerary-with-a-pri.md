# Byblos and Batroun Day Trip from Beirut: An Autumn Itinerary with a Private Driver

October is a good month to explore the Lebanese coast. The summer crowds have thinned and the sea air is milder. Travel guides often name autumn (September to November) as one of the two best seasons to visit Byblos, along with spring. Our passengers often ask us for a **taxi to Byblos**, and the most popular plan combines Byblos and Batroun in one day.

Below is a relaxed itinerary, plus some practical tips. Please check current travel advice for your nationality before you plan your trip.

## Suggested Itinerary

### Morning: Pickup in Beirut

We can collect you from anywhere in the city. Typical starting points are [Beirut – Downtown / Achrafieh](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) and [Beirut – Hamra / Verdun / Ras Beirut](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). If you have just landed, we can also meet you at [Beirut Airport (BEY)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) and drive you straight up the coast.

### Late Morning: Byblos (Jbeil)

[Byblos (Jbeil)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) is among the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. Plan to see:

- **The archaeological site and the Crusader castle.** Allow time for a slow walk.
- **The old souk.** Good for browsing and souvenirs.
- **The old harbor.** Stop here for fresh lemonade or a seafood lunch.

### Afternoon: Batroun

Continue north to [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). You can:

- Walk along the old Phoenician sea wall.
- Explore the old souks and the town's churches, including St. Stephen's.
- Stop for a coffee, or try the local lemonade.

### Evening: Dinner and the Return

Batroun has a lively evening scene with restaurants, pubs and bars. If you plan to enjoy it, leave the driving to us. Tell us your return time, and your driver will be ready. You can also book us for a late-night ride back to Beirut, Jounieh or elsewhere.

## Extending Your Trip

If you have more days, we cover many other destinations:

- [Jounieh / Kaslik / Zouk](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) for coastal dining and nightlife.
- [Tripoli](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) for its old souks and sweets.
- [Baalbek](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) and [Zahle](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) for Roman temples and Bekaa wine country.
- [Saida (Sidon)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) and [Tyre (Sour)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) for the south.
- [Faraya / Kfardebian](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) for mountain views.
