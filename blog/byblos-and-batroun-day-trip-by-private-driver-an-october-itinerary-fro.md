# Byblos and Batroun Day Trip by Private Driver: An October Itinerary from Beirut

October is a good month to see the Lebanese coast. The summer crowds have thinned and the sea air is mild. The light is good for photos of old stone harbours. A day trip from Beirut to Byblos and Batroun is one of the easiest routes in the country. It is also one of the best with a **Lebanon private driver**, because you control the pace and don't have to think about parking or directions.

Here is a simple itinerary, with practical notes on getting around.

## Morning: Arrive in Byblos (Jbeil)

Start with a **taxi to Byblos**. Byblos is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. Plan two to three hours here.

- **The archaeological site and Crusader castle.** Walk through layers of Phoenician, Roman and Crusader history.
- **The old souk.** Look for handmade crafts and small shops along the stone lanes.
- **The old harbour.** Stop for a coffee and watch the fishing boats.

Book your ride to [Byblos (Jbeil)](https://andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) from anywhere in Beirut. Pickups include [Beirut – Downtown / Achrafieh](https://andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) and [Beirut – Hamra / Verdun / Ras Beirut](https://andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator).

## Midday: Continue to Batroun

From Byblos, Batroun is a short drive up the coast. It is known for its pebble beaches, old churches, its old souks and its relaxed seaside atmosphere. Here is what to do:

- Walk through the old town and the souks.
- Have a seafood lunch near the water.
- Visit the coastal spots nearby, such as Anfeh and the Chekka area, if you have time.

Your driver can wait while you eat, or drop you and return at a time you set. Just tell us your plan when you book. See the [Batroun](https://andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) option in our estimator.
