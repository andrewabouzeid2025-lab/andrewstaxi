# Autumn Day Trip from Beirut: Byblos and Batroun by Private Taxi

October is one of the best months to travel in Lebanon. The summer crowds thin out and the heat eases. The coast north of Beirut is at its most relaxed. If you have one free day, a **taxi to Byblos** and onward to Batroun is hard to beat.

### Late Morning: Byblos (Jbeil)

[Byblos (Jbeil)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) is one of the oldest continuously inhabited towns in the world. Allow two to three hours for:

- The Phoenician and Roman ruins and the seaside castle
- A walk through the old souk
- Coffee or an early lunch by the harbour

Autumn is a comfortable season for walking the old town. Cooler temperatures also make the ruins much easier to explore.

### Afternoon: Batroun

From Byblos, continue to [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). It's a short drive up the coast, and the town is known for:

- Its old souks and seaside atmosphere
- Local restaurants, cafés and seafood by the water
- A quieter pace in autumn, with fewer visitors than in summer

### Evening: Dinner, Then Back to Beirut

Stay for sunset and dinner. Your driver can wait or return at the time you choose. The drive back is easy, with no need to worry about driving after a long day or after a drink. Our service runs around the clock, so a late return is no problem.
